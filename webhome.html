WHAT WE DO

Consultancy Management

Design of Business Plan & Investment Plan: We undertake to support our clients who need to raise loan portfolios through applying for loans in different financial institutions and supporting those who are applying for RURA licensing services and investment certificates at RDB.

Design of fundraising and grant proposal: We are committed to supporting individuals, national and international organizations seeking grants to implement their programmatic projects.

Market survey and value chain analysis: We are committed to designing solutions for communities through conducting market research. This helps us gain insight into certain situations or problems. We conduct both quantitative and qualitative data collection. This is achieved through conducting step-by-step research methodology. Our research design starts from questionnaire designing, sampling, data collection, analysis, reporting, and validation.

Monitoring and Evaluation: Nowadays, measuring the impact of a project requires a robust monitoring and evaluation approach. We do project monitoring and evaluation from baseline to end line.

Translation and Interpretation: We translate official and internal documents and provide services of both consecutive and simultaneous interpretation in five languages: French, English, Kinyarwanda, Spanish, and German.
